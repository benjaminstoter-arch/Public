From numbers to decisions.

Upload a management accounts CSV. Get the key variances, basic finance checks and a short management summary. No spreadsheets to rework, no invented reasons for the numbers.

1. Load your data

Start with the fictional example or upload your own CSV. File processing happens in this browser only. No server upload, accounts connection or external AI call.

Download example CSV

Required columns: Month, Line item, Type, Actual, Budget, Prior year. One line per month and item. Type = Revenue, Direct costs or Operating costs. Amounts in GBP, costs entered as positive numbers, no commas inside amounts. Maximum 2 MB / 5,000 rows.
