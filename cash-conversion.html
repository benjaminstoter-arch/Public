Three questions before an offer

1. Are debtors recoverable or is profit overstated?

2. Is working capital seasonal or deliberately managed around completion?

3. Does the agreed peg reflect normal trading and what is included in the definition?

The price calculation is EV less net debt plus actual closing NWC less the agreed peg. This assumes a cash-free, debt-free deal and no other completion adjustments. Cash and debt definitions must be agreed in the SPA. Numbers are fictional and do not represent advice.
